<ul class="list-disc pl-6 mt-4">
<li>two months, if the total period of employment with the employer is at least two years but less than four years;</li>
<li>three months, if the total period of employment is at least four years but less than six years;</li>
<li>four months, if the total period of employment is at least six years but less than eight years;</li>

<ul class="list-disc pl-6 mt-4">
<li>when the employee fully or partly resumes their work; or</li>
<li>when the employee would have resumed their work according to the application for parental leave that applies when notice of termination is given. (SFS 2015:759).</li>
